LauncherX is a local-first launcher. There is no account to create, and your home screen layout, settings and personalization data stay on your device by default. This policy explains what information is involved, how it is used, and how you can stay in control.

1. Information We Collect and Process

1.1 No account, no identity data

You do not need to register or sign in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, passwords, contacts, messages, photos, or precise location.

1.2 Data stored only on your device (never uploaded by us)

  • Home screen & personalization: pages and grid layout, Dock, folders, custom app groups, hidden apps, custom icons and labels.
  • Preferences: grid size, icon size/shape, font scaling, gesture mappings, night mode, search provider, ad/purchase toggles, and similar settings.
  • Wallpaper: the preset you choose, or a custom image that is copied into the app's private storage so it persists across restarts.
  • Subscription cache: a local flag recording whether Plus is active, used to keep your entitlement during the offline grace period.
  • Backups: manual exports are saved to a location you pick; automatic backups are written to the app's external private directory and are rotated so that at most the latest 3 are kept.

Uninstalling the app deletes this private data; clearing app storage in Android Settings does the same.

Sensitive permissions are optional and must be granted by you in system settings; you can revoke them at any time.

The services below are provided by Google. We enable analytics, crash reporting and advertising only as needed, and we gate analytics/crash/advertising on your consent where required. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies.

We do not sell your personal information, nor share it for third parties' own marketing purposes.

3. Storage and Security

  • Local data lives in the app's private directory and is isolated by the Android app sandbox.
  • We do not operate an account system and do not store your launcher data on our own servers.
  • Ad/analytics data is stored by Google on its infrastructure, which may be located outside your country or region.
  • Local data is retained until you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

4. Your Rights and Choices

  • Turn off personalized ads in the Google Settings "Ads" page, or reset/delete your advertising ID.
  • Revoke permissions (notification access, accessibility, usage access) at any time in system settings.
  • Erase local data by clearing app storage or uninstalling.
  • Access, correct and delete your data directly in the app; contact us for any other requests.
  • Manage your subscription and cancel Plus at any time in Google Play → Subscriptions.

5. Children

LauncherX is intended for a general audience, is not directed to children, and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a minor, please use the app under the guidance of a parent or guardian. If we learn that we have collected a child's information without guardian consent, we will delete it promptly. Guardians with questions are welcome to contact us.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated in-app or prominently on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
