ZapReels — Data Deletion Instructions

Applies to the Android app ZapReels (com.nq.ZapReels). Last updated: October 9, 2026.

ZapReels is usable without creating an account. We identify your installation by a random anonymous device ID — we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number. This page explains how to delete the data associated with your installation.

Two ways to delete your data
  1. Immediately, on your device — clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. This removes all data stored on your phone.
  2. Server-side deletion request — email nqhl259@gmail.com and we will delete the records our backend holds for your installation.

1. Delete data stored on your device (immediate, no request needed)

All locally stored data lives in the app's private storage. You can erase it at any time:

  • Android Settings → Apps → ZapReels → Storage → Clear storage (sometimes labelled “Clear data”), or
  • Uninstall the app (long-press the ZapReels icon → Uninstall).

This permanently deletes:

  • your anonymous device ID and app preferences (language, diamond balance, booster quota, check-in/task counters, ad-frequency counters);
  • your local watch history and favourites, cached episode data, and your unlock progress (unlockIndex);
  • any analytics events still waiting in the on-device upload queue.

After clearing, the app behaves like a fresh installation and a new anonymous device ID is generated. Note that clearing local storage does not delete records that were already sent to our backend or to the third-party services listed in §4 — use §2 for those.

2. Request deletion of server-side data

Email nqhl259@gmail.com with the subject “Data Deletion Request” and include:

  • the anonymous device ID of your installation, if you have it;
  • if you do not have the device ID: your approximate install date, country, app language and app version (Settings → Version);
  • any detail that helps us locate the record (for example, a subscription or in-app purchase you made).

Because the app is anonymous, the device ID is the only unique key we hold; if you cannot provide it we will attempt to locate your record from the details above and will tell you if we cannot identify it uniquely.

What happens next

  • We acknowledge your request within 7 days.
  • We complete deletion within 30 days of verifying the request (or within the period required by your local law).
  • We confirm by email once the deletion is done.

Please note: deletion is irreversible. Your watch history, favourites, unlock progress, task/check-in progress and any unused booster quota associated with that installation will be lost and cannot be restored.

3. What we delete, and what we may keep

On a verified request we delete (or irreversibly anonymise) the records we hold for your installation, including:

  • your anonymous device ID and the usage/behavioural events linked to it (dramas viewed, watch progress, unlocks, tasks, check-in, ad impressions);
  • your server-side profile state (diamond balance snapshot, booster quota, task progress);
  • your push token, if you had notifications enabled.

We may retain, where the law requires it or where we need it to defend legal claims:

  • aggregated, anonymised statistics that can no longer be linked to you;
  • purchase and tax records — these are held by Google Play as merchant of record, not by us, and we cannot delete them;
  • records we must keep for fraud prevention, security or accounting obligations, for the period the law prescribes.

Our standard retention period for usage/behavioural data is up to 24 months. See the Privacy Policy (§6 Data Retention, §9 Your Rights) for details.

4. Data held by third-party services

Some data is processed independently by our service providers, each under its own privacy policy. Deleting it requires a request to that provider (or using the controls it offers):

  • Google — Firebase Analytics / Remote Config / Crashlytics / Cloud Messaging, Google AdMob and Google Play Billing: Google data & privacy controls; ads personalisation: Ads Settings (you can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Adjust — install attribution: adjust.com privacy policy.
  • Tenjin — attribution and revenue measurement: tenjin.com/privacy.
  • Ad networks — Pangle, Liftoff/Vungle and InMobi (mediated through AdMob): see their respective privacy policies for opt-out and deletion tools.
  • Meta / Facebook — install-referrer decryption via the Facebook SDK. ZapReels does not offer Facebook Login, so no Facebook account is linked to your installation. You can control Meta's own off-app data in Facebook → Off-Facebook Activity.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the app, contact us at the address below and we will delete the associated data.
