Completing advertiser tasks in the Offerwall may share your advertising ID and device data with Tapjoy and the advertiser under their policies.

Local diamond balance is maintained as mirror = server snapshot + pendingDelta. The pending delta is never cleared by a server snapshot (server does not track local spend) and is restored at cold start, so spending or offline grants are not “revived” by a later server refresh.
