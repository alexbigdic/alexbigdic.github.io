ZapReels Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Device information: device ID, operating system version, app version, language and locale settings, device model, and network type.
  • Usage data: dramas viewed, watch progress, episodes unlocked, tasks completed, check-in history, and interactions with advertisements.
  • Account data: if you create an account, we collect your user ID, email address (if provided), and any profile information you choose to share.
  • Advertising identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) or equivalent device-level advertising identifier, used for ad delivery and attribution.

2. How We Collect Information

We collect information: (a) automatically when you use the App through our service providers and SDKs; (b) when you voluntarily provide information, such as when you contact support; and (c) through our third-party analytics and advertising partners.

3. Third-Party SDKs

The App integrates the following categories of third-party SDKs:

  • Analytics: Firebase Analytics (Google), Adjust — to understand app usage, measure campaign performance, and attribute installs.
  • Advertising: AdMob (Google) — to serve and measure advertisements within the App.
  • Messaging: Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — to deliver push notifications.
  • Crash reporting: Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — to monitor app stability and diagnose issues.

Each third-party partner processes data under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review those policies.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to: operate the drama catalog and video player; compute and track in-app rewards; serve and measure the effectiveness of advertising; detect and prevent fraud; improve the App and user experience; comply with legal obligations; and respond to support inquiries.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data: (a) with the third-party SDK providers listed above for the purposes described; (b) with law enforcement or regulatory authorities when required by law; (c) in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets; and (d) with your consent or at your direction.

6. Data Retention

Usage and behavioral data is retained for up to 24 months. Aggregated and anonymized analytics may be retained indefinitely. Account data is retained until you delete your account or request its deletion. We will delete or anonymize your data upon your verified request, subject to any legal obligations to retain certain information.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; request correction of inaccurate data; request deletion of your data; restrict or object to processing; receive your data in a portable format; and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@zapreels.app. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

9. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy within the App. Your continued use of the App after the effective date constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at privacy@zapreels.app.
